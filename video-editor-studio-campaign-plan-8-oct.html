Meta rebuild plan · Oct 8, 2026

Meta Rebuild Plan
Focus. Prove. Then scale.

We are rebuilding the Meta account from scratch. Instead of four campaigns mixing every country, there will be two: one for the US, and one for the UK, Canada, Australia and Germany. The ads focus on the products that sell best in your Shopify store, using the ads that already brought sales or carts, plus new ads for best sellers that have never had an ad.

TL;DR
  • WhatWe pause the current 4 campaigns and relaunch as 2: one for the US, one for the UK, Canada, Australia and Germany. At $100 a day, that's $60 and $40.
  • WhyThe current setup spreads about $48 a day over 176 active ads in 8 ad sets. No ad set has had enough sales to learn, so there is nothing to lose by pausing, and the tracking fixes need new ad sets anyway.
  • ProductsThe 10 best sellers in Shopify, including 3 that sell well but have never had an ad: Explainer, Cinematic and Tracker Callout.
  • AdsThe 7 strongest ads we already have (they brought 18 of Meta's 22 sales), 1 existing video to test, and 27 new ads covering all 10 products: 35 in total.
  • What we keepYour pixel history, the best-selling audience, and the original posts with their likes and comments. Old campaigns are paused, not deleted.
Why rebuild

Why we start again instead of fixing what's there

In short The current setup is spread too thin to learn, and the fixes we need can only be made in new ad sets.

Campaigns4
Ad sets8
Active ads176

01

The budget is spread too thin to learn

  • Over the last 14 days the account spent about $48 a day across 176 active ads. That is about $0.27 per ad per day, so most ads never get a real test.
  • Meta needs around 50 purchases a week in one ad set to finish learning. The whole account had 22 purchases in 25 days, so no ad set has ever finished learning.
  • That means pausing the current ad sets loses nothing. Two campaigns with one ad set each give every dollar a better chance to teach Meta who buys.
02

The fixes need new ad sets

  • Counting by clicks only (7-day click) can't be switched on in existing ad sets. Meta locks it when an ad set is created.
  • The country split, product browsing off, and each ad opening its own product page all go in cleanly at launch.
  • Every link gets tracking tags, including Triple Whale's, so the new results can be checked against your store from day one.
03

A fresh start doesn't erase what Meta knows

  • Most of what Meta has learned lives in your pixel (who visited and who bought), your audiences, and your posts. Pausing campaigns erases none of it, and the new campaigns use it from day one.
  • We already took what the old campaigns taught us: the ads that sold, the products that sell, and the markets that buy. They are all in this plan.
04

The risk is small

  • Your store-side tools (Triple Whale and your dashboard) show the current setup bringing back about $0.35 to $0.51 for every $1 spent. A short learning period puts little revenue at risk.
  • The old campaigns are paused, not deleted, so we keep their history to compare against and can switch back if needed.
What we carry over
  • Your pixel history: purchases and visitors stay with the account.
  • The best audience: the 1% lookalike of past buyers brought 14 of Meta's 22 sales. It guides both new campaigns.
  • The original posts: the 8 kept ads run from their existing posts, so their likes and comments come with them.
How we switch
  • Build both campaigns paused, and check every link, headline and tag.
  • Launch at the start of a day and pause all old campaigns the same day, so old and new never compete for the same people.
  • No edits for the first 7 days: each edit restarts learning. We judge results at day 14.

In week one, Meta's reported sales will likely look lower. Part of that is Meta no longer counting people who only saw an ad, not fewer real sales.

Budget

Budget, and how we judge it

In short 60/40 for two weeks, then move money toward whichever market earns it.

Split at $100 a day

US $60 · UK/CA/AU/DE $40

At about $83 per sale, $40 a day gives Campaign 2 around 7 sales in two weeks. That is enough to judge it. With less it would take a month to know.

Target cost per sale

Under ~$90 (US) · under ~$75 (UK/CA/AU/DE)

This is what keeps each market at your 1.3x minimum, based on its average order ($118 and $99). We use Meta's click-only count, then check the orders against Shopify.

Every weekMove $10 toward a campaign that is under its target and has at least one Meta order that Shopify can also see.
After two weeksIf UK/CA/AU/DE hits its target: keep 60/40 or go 50/50. If not: move to 70/30 or 80/20.
Age at day 14If 65+ still takes about 10% of spend with no add-to-carts, or 25–34 stays below break-even, we narrow the age range.
GermanyIf it is still the weakest of the four after two weeks, we drop it and give its share to the UK, Canada and Australia.
Setup

How the new campaigns are set up

One ad set per campaign, with all 35 adsThe 7 kept ads, the Content Creator video and the 27 new ones in a single ad set, so every sale teaches the same ad set. Meta moves spend toward the ads that sell.
Age: minimum 25, suggested 30–64People aged 35–64 bought 10 of the 12 sales from clicks and 91% of the revenue (Sep 11 – Oct 8). 25–34 spent $218 for one $99 sale, and 65+ spent $101 with no add-to-cart. Industry data agrees that editors are mostly 25–44 (median age 33–37). The minimum age of 25 is a strict rule. The 30–64 range is a suggestion: Meta starts there but can still find buyers outside it. We check the age split again at day 14.
Optimized for the number of purchasesMeta looks for as many buyers as possible. Optimizing for order value needs more sales a week than we have at launch. We revisit it once there are enough sales, or if the average order drops.
Each ad opens its own product pageSo the person lands on the product they clicked, not a different one.
Product browsing offIt showed cheap single packs under the ad and pulled the average order down from $174 to $83.
Automatic creative changes offMeta will show the ads as designed, without auto-adding music, filters or other images.
Counting by clicks only (7-day click), with your OKCloser to what Shopify and Triple Whale see. See the Tracking & Attribution Read.
Tracking tags on every adIncluding Triple Whale's tags, so Triple Whale can match sales to each ad (suggested in the Tracking Read).
